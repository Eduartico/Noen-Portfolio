    Tell me the rest

    Before you order

    Changes
    Two rounds of changes are included. A new design after the paint is approved counts as a new commission.
    Complexity
    Prices cover the gear in the gallery. Detailed emblems, camo and custom parts are quoted before you pay.
    Locations
    I don't have every planet or ship. Tell me the place you want and I'll say what I can build, or what comes close.
    References
    Send drawings, screenshots or a clear description. If someone else designed the character, include their permission.
    Payment
    Up to €100 is paid before I start. Above that, half before I start and half before the final files. PayPal, arranged by DM.
    Timing
    A trooper takes up to 7 days once the references are complete and the payment is in. Videos take up to 10 days and scenes up to 14.
    What you get
    The finished image or video at full size. Models, textures and scene files stay with me.
    Use
    Post it, print it for yourself or use it as a banner, with credit. Commercial use and reselling are not allowed.
    Refunds
    Full refund before I start, and half until the first paint preview. After the preview there are no refunds. EU buyers keep their legal right of withdrawal.
    Portfolio
    I may show the finished piece here and on my socials. Ask before I start if you want it private.
    Requests
    I can turn down any request, including anything I can't do well.
